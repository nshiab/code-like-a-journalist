# Code Like a Journalist

![Illustration of two computational journalists working together.](/assets/sharing-v1.jpg)

Hi! 👋

My name is Nael Shiab. I am a [computational journalist](https://www.naelshiab.com/) based in Montreal. I work for [CBC News](https://www.cbc.ca/news) as a Senior Data Producer.

This is a free, open-source online course on TypeScript and data analysis, available in English and French. You don't need any experience with data or code: I guide you step by step, from the fundamentals of TypeScript to complete data projects.

You'll first learn the language and the tools you need to work with it. Then, using the [Simple Data Analysis (SDA)](https://github.com/nshiab/simple-data-analysis) library, you'll learn how to acquire, analyze, visualize, and automate data. By the end, you'll have the skills to investigate questions with data and build reliable, reproducible workflows of your own.

Over the years, I have learned so much from free tutorials and open-source projects maintained by passionate people. My hope is that this course will help others in the same way.

Thanks to the support of the [Michener Awards Foundation](https://www.michenerawards.ca/media-release/michener-awards-foundation-announces-its-2024-fellowships-winners/), I took several months off from my current job to fully focus on building the course. I could also count on the support of [Jean-Hugues Roy](https://github.com/jhroy), data journalist at La Presse and former journalism professor at UQAM.

To allow teachers and professors to reuse the course with their students, the content is released under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Public License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/?ref=chooser-v1). Just give an appropriate credit with my name and a link to the [website](https://www.code-like-a-journalist.com/). 😊
